CAKE Placements & Visits provide an opportunity to step outside your usual working environment, connect with other researchers and exchange knowledge. 

Whether you're developing an existing collaboration, exploring a new connection or visiting an international partner, a little planning can help you get the most out of the experience.

**Learn more and apply: [https://www.cake.ac.uk/about/placements/](https://www.cake.ac.uk/about/placements/)**

## Code of Conduct 

Make sure the experience is a positive one for everyone involved: 

- Use respectful, inclusive and professional language in all communications.
- Be open to learning, asking questions and sharing your own expertise.
- Be mindful of cultural differences, disciplinary backgrounds and varying levels of experience.
- Communicate clearly about logistics, expectations, boundaries and support needs.
- Remember that successful knowledge exchange is collaborative, flexible and mutually beneficial.

Everyone involved in the CAKE network must abide by the [CAKE Code of Conduct](../code-of-conduct.md), helping to ensure a supportive and respectful environment for all.

**Enjoy your placement or visit and have fun!**
