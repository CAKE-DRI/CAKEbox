This document outlines the terms of reference for the organisation and delivery of an equality, diversity and inclusion (EDI) working group collaboration between the [Computational Abilities Knowledge Exchange](https://cake-dri.github.io/) (CAKE) and [Computational Science Centre for Research Communities](https://www.cosec.ac.uk/) (CoSeC) projects.

## Purpose 
To support and advance equality, diversity and inclusion (EDI) across the UK Digital Research Infrastructure (DRI) community, particularly communities engaged with CAKE and CoSeC activities. The group brings together people with experience across different communities to share knowledge, identify common challenges and opportunities, connect communities with relevant expertise and resources, and develop practical, community-driven actions.

## Definitions 
### Equality
Fairness in treatment and access to opportunity: we must ensure that individuals, or groups of individuals, are not treated less favourably because of their protected characteristics, as defined under the Equality Act 2010. 

### Diversity
Recognition, respect and celebration of differences in background, identity and experience. A diverse environment is one with a wide range of backgrounds and mindsets.

### Inclusion 
Creating spaces where all individuals feel welcomed, respected and valued. Inclusion involves awareness of unconscious bias and intentional actions to remove exclusionary practices.

## Objectives 
The CAKE-CoSeC EDI working group will: 

* Provide a space for people working across UK DRI to exchange experiences and perspectives, identify common EDI barriers and challenges, and highlight opportunities for improvement.
* Collect, curate and disseminate relevant EDI good practices, expertise and resources to support learning and improvement across the UK DRI community.  
* Work collaboratively to develop actionable strategies and tangible initiatives to address identified EDI barriers and priorities.
* Advocate for EDI improvements by making informed recommendations to CAKE, CoSeC and relevant stakeholders.
* Liaise with relevant groups and initiatives in the community and adjacent communities to exchange approaches, best practices, and share material. For example: EDI Working Group of the Society for Research Software Engineering, Women in HPC, DiveRSE.

## Scope
The group is a collaborative and advisory forum, not an EDI authority or a substitute for the responsibilities of individual organisations, employers or funding bodies. The group will focus on practical, community-driven initiatives while aligning with the broader [EDI guidance from UKRI](https://www.ukri.org/manage-your-award/good-research-resource-hub/guidance-for-equality-diversity-and-inclusion/). It will not duplicate existing policy work but will instead apply and contextualise it to meet the specific needs of CAKE, CoSeC, and related communities. 

## Roles and Responsibilities 
The group aims to bring together people with a range of experiences, perspectives, expertise and connections across CAKE, CoSeC and the wider UK DRI community. Members contribute their own knowledge and experience and, where appropriate, insights from the communities and networks with which they engage. Members are not expected to formally represent a particular community or speak on behalf of all its members. Instead, the group draws on their diverse perspectives and connections to better understand shared challenges, exchange knowledge and identify opportunities for practical improvements.

### Chair
#### Responsibilities: 
In addition to fulfilling all responsibilities of a Member, as outlined below, Chair(s) of the CAKE-CoSeC EDI working group will:

* Organise and lead meetings of the working group, including setting agendas, preparing relevant materials and chairing discussions to facilitate inclusive and productive engagement.
* Represent the group as appropriate and ensure that contact details for the Chair(s) are publicly available.
* Help the group agree priorities, coordinate activities and progress, with responsibilities shared appropriately among Members.
* Coordinate the onboarding and offboarding of Members.
* Be responsible for removing Members who do not comply with the CAKE Code of Conduct.

The Chair(s) may delegate administrative and other responsibilities as appropriate.

#### Appointment:  
New Chair(s) will be elected through a nomination and anonymous voting process. The current Chair election process is outlined [here](chair-election-process.md).

#### Term length:  
* Chair(s) will serve a minimum term of one year and a maximum term of three years. 
* Chair terms will be staggered where possible to ensure continuity.
* Upon completing their term, Chairs may become Members of the group. 

#### Stepping down:
Chair(s) who wish to step down, regardless of term length, must either:

* Initiate the Chair election process to find a replacement, or
* Nominate another Chair or member to run the election process on their behalf.

#### Removal: 
A Chair may be removed if they are not fulfilling their role or breach the CAKE Code of Conduct.

* The process may be initiated by a formal complaint to the CAKE and/or CoSeC management team.
    * CAKE Management Team: [cake@jiscmail.ac.uk](mailto:cake@jiscmail.ac.uk) 
    * CoSeC Management: [COSEC@stfc.ac.uk](mailto:COSEC@stfc.ac.uk) 
* Members of the CAKE and CoSeC management teams who are not Members or Chairs of the working group will investigate the complaint. This may include action up to and including: 
    * Giving the Chair an opportunity to respond, including explaining any barriers to participation or mitigating circumstances. 
    * Removal of the Chair. 

### Member
#### Responsibilities: 
Members of the CAKE-CoSeC EDI working group will: 

* Engage in meetings and group activities, contributing their experience and perspectives to help identify common EDI barriers and challenges and highlight opportunities for improvement. 
* Work collaboratively to develop and deliver actions, resources, recommendations and outputs, and help to disseminate these across the UK DRI community. 
* Comply with the CAKE Code of Conduct and contribute to a respectful, inclusive and constructive working environment.

#### Appointment: 
* Individuals may self-nominate by approaching the Chair(s).
* Key communities engaging with CoSeC and CAKE are invited to nominate a representative. Nomination by a community does not imply that a Member has a formal representative role or a mandate to speak on its behalf.
* Nominees receive the terms of reference and CAKE Code of Conduct, and must confirm their ability to fulfil the role, including regularly attending meetings.

#### Term length: 
* Members serve a one-year renewable term. Once a year, the Chair(s) will invite Members to confirm whether they wish to continue and are able to contribute to the group's work.

#### Stepping down before end of term: 
* Members may step down at any time by notifying the Chair(s).

#### Removal: 
A Member may be removed if they are not fulfilling their role or breach the CAKE Code of Conduct.

* The process may be initiated by a formal complaint submitted to the Chair(s), a formal complaint submitted to the CAKE and/or CoSeC management team, or directly initiated by the Chair(s). 
    * CAKE Management Team: [cake@jiscmail.ac.uk](mailto:cake@jiscmail.ac.uk) 
    * CoSeC Management: [COSEC@stfc.ac.uk](mailto:COSEC@stfc.ac.uk) 
* The process may include action up to and including: 
    * Giving the Member an opportunity to respond, including explaining any barriers to participation or mitigating circumstances. 
    * Removal of the Member. 

## Meeting Arrangements
### Frequency: 
The group will normally meet twice a month:

* All-hands meeting: A meeting for all Members to exchange knowledge, discuss priorities, share updates and agree actions.
* Mini working group meetings: Smaller, focused meetings to progress specific activities, initiatives or outputs.

Additional ad hoc meetings may be arranged as required.

### Format: 
Regular meetings are virtual. 
 
### Quorum:
At least 50% of Members must be present for decisions to proceed. In the absence of a consensus, the Chair(s) make the final decision. 

### Access and participation: 
Meetings are closed to non-Members, unless invited to attend. 

### Minutes

* Meeting minutes will be shared with Members to record key discussions, decisions and agreed actions.
* Public summaries may be produced and shared to communicate the group's activities, outcomes and learning with the wider community. These summaries will be anonymised and will not attribute comments to individuals without their explicit agreement.
    
## Authorship of Outputs 

Outputs may be developed by the group to support EDI knowledge exchange across UK DRI communities.

Authorship and contributions will be agreed for each output, taking into account the nature of the work and the contributions made. Members may contribute to outputs through drafting, reviewing, sharing expertise, providing community perspectives or supporting dissemination. Contributions will be acknowledged appropriately, and named authorship will reflect substantive contributions to the development of the output.

Outputs may be developed by the group as a whole, by smaller working groups or by individual Members and invited contributors. Where an output is published in the name of the group, the relevant contributors will seek appropriate input and agreement from the group before publication. Group endorsement should be distinguished from the views of individual authors or contributors, and no output should be assumed to represent the views of all Members unless this has been explicitly agreed.

Members are not expected to contribute to every output, and participation in the group does not automatically imply authorship or endorsement. 

## Ethics
### Code of Conduct
* All Chair(s) and Members are expected to follow the CAKE Code of Conduct: [https://www.cake.ac.uk/CAKEbox/code-of-conduct/](https://www.cake.ac.uk/CAKEbox/code-of-conduct/)
* In summary, Chair(s) and Members should act with respect, professionalism, and integrity at all times. Discriminatory, harassing, or exclusionary behaviour will not be tolerated. Chair(s) and Members are expected to foster a supportive, inclusive environment where all voices are heard and valued. 
 
### Conflict of Interest Disclosure: 
* Any potential or actual conflicts of interest must be disclosed to the Chair(s) promptly. These will be managed transparently to maintain the group’s integrity and impartiality.
* We follow the [UKRI definition of a conflict of interest](https://www.ukri.org/who-we-are/how-we-are-governed/conflicts-of-interests/), which states:
    * *A conflict of interest is a situation in which an individual’s ability to exercise judgment or act in one role is, could be, or is seen to be impaired or otherwise influenced by their involvement in another role or relationship.*

### Confidentiality of Sensitive Discussions
* Chair(s) and Members must treat sensitive discussions as confidential, especially those involving personal experiences or identity-related matters. While meeting minutes and outcomes will be shared publicly, sensitive content will be anonymised or withheld to protect individuals’ privacy.
