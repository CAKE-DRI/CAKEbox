### Annual review of the working group

We are one year into this group, so it’s time to review! The purpose is to review how we’ve evolved and re-focus to help us achieve value and impact moving forward.

#### Overview: 

Membership:

* 23 members at the beginning 
* 4 members left
* 6 members joined

We have a relatively large and diverse group, with a range of interests, experience and levels of available time.

When we first set up the group, we had a lot of members and a lot of different interests.
We established a structure of one monthly all-group meeting and smaller working groups to take forward areas of interest: 

* Allow people to work on areas they were interested in.
* Allow more voices to contribute.
* Make participation more flexible around people's other commitments.
* The working groups were intended to be open to all members. Members didn't need to formally "sign up" to one stream and could move between areas depending on their interests and capacity.

_Community Outreach:_

* Purpose: To connect the working group with the real needs of the communities we want to support. 
* Progress: Developed a survey to understand community priorities and how people currently engage with EDI resources. Closes 14th October. Goal is to use this information to guide our future efforts. 

_Measuring Impact:_

* Purpose: Understand what is working and what is not in EDI practice, defining metrics for “EDI success” (KPIs) and accountability.
* Progress: Discussion around terminology, stakeholders and accountability. Investigation into HR practices and metrics as a source for KPIs. (e.g. how EDI data is collected, can numbers of EDI complaints and grievances be used as a metric, action points)

_Topic-Based Guides:_

* Purpose: Identifying, collecting and curating existing resources on specific topics. Not to reinvent, but to translate and adapt so they are directly applicable, accessible and actionable to our community
* Progress: Actively collating and reviewing guides and online resources on relevant topics. Developing analysis criteria 

#### Discussion summary 

* We have a unique opportunity with so many great people, perspectives and communities represented in this group. 
* We are people working in the DRI community, connected into different networks and communities, and with direct experience of what is working well and what isn't. We are in a good position to: 
    * Identify issues and needs across our communities.
    * Share experiences and learn from one another.
    * Identify realistic actions that could be taken forward.
    * Connect people with relevant knowledge, expertise and resources.
    * Bring issues and recommendations to the attention of CAKE, CoSeC and other groups.
* Given the challenges of working in this space and voluntarily, our progress has been good! The step now is articulating our progress towards longer term outcomes 
* The mini working groups have value to help us focus on outputs and move forward from just discussion. 

Things to improve upon: 

* Lack of connection of the mini working groups to the wider group, so when it comes to approving outputs or getting guidance/advice, it’s difficult for the wider group to be useful.
* Lack of SMART objectives for the mini working groups. This makes it difficult for new people to join and pick up actions.
* Making the group worthwhile for all volunteers. People have their personal motivations and goals for joining the group, how do we connect with that and align?
* Authorship of outputs. 

_Next steps: see Actions_

Reminder that the survey outcome will guide what we are doing in the group going forward, steer from the community.
