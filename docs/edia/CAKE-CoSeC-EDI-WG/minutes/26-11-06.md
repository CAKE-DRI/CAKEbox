### Annual Review: Outcomes & Progress 

**Authorship of outputs:** definition has been included in the TOR revision. 

**Making the group worthwhile for all volunteers:**

* Added a regular section to the agenda for knowledge sharing, community feedback and issues to be raised. Goal is to ensure that we are addressing volunteer priorities and aligning with interests. 
* Still ongoing, welcoming any ideas 

**Mini working groups** 

* Issue: 
    * Lack of connection of the mini working groups to the wider group, so when it comes to approving outputs or getting guidance/advice, it’s difficult for the wider group to be useful.
    * Lack of SMART objectives for the mini working groups. This makes it difficult for new people to join and pick up actions.

* Proposal: 
    * Use a SMART framework (example shared) to rescope all mini working group activities 
    * Each mini working group will fill out the framework - maintaining it as a live document and action tracker. 
    * When reporting progress and actions in all-hands meetings, this document will be used. 
    * Expect this to evolve as we go 

* Comments: 
    * Do we still need the working groups? Can we use the framework to spin off smaller packets of work when they make sense? 


### Community Outreach Survey: Preliminary Results 

Analysis is ongoing. 

* Launched the survey in September, closed 7th October. Received 24 responses.  
* Important points/topics:
    * Class/socioeconomic status is potentially missing in our scope
    * Equity vs equality could be a discussion with our group 
    * Guidance on handling of inappropriate behaviour or conflict
    * Guidance on inclusive leadership and management 
    * Guidance on recruitment and hiring processes 
* Lots of great examples of positive impact 
* Interesting spread of where people get their EDI guidance from
* Value in templates or example policies - drop and go things to help people implement better practice. 

Comments: 

* Interesting to see different priorities from the community to what we assumed. 
* Only 24 responses, can we put a metric on the quality of the data? How representative are they? 
* Next steps? 
    * Formal output presenting our results 
    * Shows us what we can practically do within this group.
